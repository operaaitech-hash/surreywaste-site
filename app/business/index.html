Who it is for

Anyone who clears waste more than once a month.

Built with the people Surrey Waste already collects for. If your waste is currently a WhatsApp to a driver and a pile of paper for the accountant, this is the fix.

Contractors and fit-out firms

Strip-out waste, plasterboard, rubble, offcuts. Book from the site as the skip would have filled, without the skip on the road for a week.

Garages and workshops

Exhausts, radiators, cats, batteries sorted out, old parts and packaging. Scrap paid on the spot, the rest cleared on the same visit.

Property and facilities managers

End-of-tenancy clearances, bulky items from communal areas, office churn. Every site on one account, every job with a photo before and after.

What the account does

Book like a ride. Account like a company.

  • Team logins. Site managers book, the office approves if you want it to, finance sees the invoice.
  • One monthly invoice, itemised by site and job, with every collection's photos attached.
  • Commercial prices on every load, and 10% off when a site is collected fortnightly or more often.
  • Compliance filed for you. Carrier registration, waste transfer notes and disposal records on every job, exportable in one click when the auditor asks.
  • Weigh & Pay statements per van, per month, paid by bank transfer to the account you nominate.
  • Standing collections. Same site, same window, every week or fortnight, without rebooking.
