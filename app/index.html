The old way

Getting rid of stuff is still a phone call.

A pile in the garage, a strip-out on site, a van full of copper offcuts. In 2026 you still ring round for a price, wait in for a van, and hope the person who turns up is licensed.

01

Hire a skip

A permit if it is on the road, a week on the drive, and you do all the loading. For most jobs it is the wrong size, in both directions.

02

Message a man with a van

A Facebook group, a price that changes on the doorstep, no waste transfer note. If it ends up in a lay-by, the fine lands on you.

03

Drive to the scrap yard

Queue, unload, argue about grades, then a cheque or a transfer days later. Your afternoon gone for a few hundred pounds of copper.

Clearby is the fourth option. A licensed van, a fixed price, and a record of where everything went.

How it works

Three taps. One van.

Built around the way you already book a ride: say what and where, see the price, watch it arrive. No quotes by email, no waiting in all day.

1

Tap what needs clearing

Rubbish, site waste or scrap metal. Drop the pin where the pile is, add a photo if it helps the driver.

2

See the price, pick a window

Six load sizes, one price each, loading included. Choose a day and a two-hour window and confirm. You pay the driver by card on the day, once the van is loaded.

3

Track the van, pay on the day

Watch the van come to you. The driver loads it all, you pay by card on the day, and the receipt and waste transfer note land in your jobs.

Inside the app

One app, two sides.

Customers book and track. Drivers run their day. Pick a side and scroll: every screen here is the Clearby app itself, feature by feature.

  1. 01

    Sign in in ten seconds

    Tell the app you are a customer, add your name and mobile number, and you are on the map. Email is optional and only used for receipts.

  2. 02

    The map is home

    The app opens on a live map with the Clearby vans out on the road. Allow your location and it shows the vans near you, with rubbish, scrap metal, or both in one visit underneath.

  3. 03

    Three taps to book

    What it is, how much there is, and when. Every load size shows its price as you tap it, loading included, so you see the full price before you confirm.

  4. 04

    Photos and notes for the driver

    Drop the pin where the van should stop and add a couple of photos of the pile. Gate codes, stairs and where to park go in the notes.

  5. 05

    A two-hour window

    Pick a day and a two-hour window. Your booking is read back with the price before you confirm, and the driver calls 30 minutes before arriving.

  6. 06

    Watch the van come to you

    Once a driver has your job you see their name, the van registration and their rating. The van moves on the map as it drives to you, and you can call or message from the same screen.

  7. 07

    A bigger pile is your call

    If there is more than you booked, the driver sets the next size and the new price appears in your app. Nothing is loaded until you tap Approve, so the price never goes up quietly.

  8. 08

    Receipts kept in Jobs

    Every collection stays in Jobs with its timeline and receipt. The waste transfer note follows when the office closes the job, so you have a record of where your waste went.

  9. 09

    Weigh & Pay for scrap

    Tap your metals and give a rough weight to see an indicative band. The driver weighs and grades it on the van's scales in front of you, then pays you by bank transfer, never cash.

  10. 10

    Business accounts

    Switch on a business account for trade prices on every load and one invoice a month. Book fortnightly or more often and regular collections take 10% off every job.

  1. 01

    Today's run, in slot order

    Your day opens on the next job, with the address, the window and the customer's notes. The rest of the run sits below it in the order you drive it.

  2. 02

    Know the job before you get there

    Each job carries the access notes, the customer's photos, the size they booked and the agreed price. Navigate and Call are one tap away.

  3. 03

    One tap moves the job on

    Set off, arrived, loading, done. The button at the bottom is always the next step, the customer sees each change in their app, and you give them a call 30 minutes out.

  4. 04

    Kerb photos, before and after

    Photograph the pile before you load and the empty spot after. A clear before and after settles most disputes before they start.

  5. 05

    Step up the size on the doorstep

    If the pile is bigger than booked, pick the size it really fills. The customer approves the new price in their app before anything goes in the van.

  6. 06

    Weigh each metal

    On Weigh & Pay jobs, weigh each metal in front of the customer and set its grade. The app works out the rate and the payout, and shows the account the bank transfer goes to.

  7. 07

    Transfer note on the spot

    Issue the waste transfer note from the job once the load is on. Carrier, address and waste are filled in for you, and Mark done only unlocks once the note exists.

  8. 08

    Saved on the phone

    Jobs, weights and photos are stored on the phone as you work, so a dead spot in a block of flats does not lose anything. Finished jobs stay on the day they were closed.

  9. 09

    Earnings by job, paid Fridays

    Earnings shows what each job paid: a fixed fee for the load size plus 10% of the scrap you buy. The week's total goes to your bank account by transfer every Friday.

Questions

Straight answers.

Is Clearby a different company to Surrey Waste?

No. Clearby is the app Surrey Waste is building so that booking a collection feels like booking a ride. The vans, the drivers, the licences and the prices are Surrey Waste's. The app simply removes the phone call.

When does it launch?

iOS and Android first, across Surrey, Middlesex and London, then wider as more drivers join. Leave your details above and you get the link the day it is live. Until then you can book the same jobs on surreywaste.com.

How is the price fixed before anyone has seen the pile?

You pick one of six load sizes, each with one price that includes loading. The driver checks the size on arrival. If the pile is bigger than you booked, the app shows the next size up and you approve it before anything is loaded. It never goes up quietly.

Why can't you pay cash for scrap?

The Scrap Metal Dealers Act 2013 bans cash for scrap in the UK. Every Clearby purchase is paid by bank transfer, with ID checked and a purchase statement saved in the app. It protects you as much as us.

What can't you take?

Asbestos, fridges and freezers, tyres, batteries, paint and chemicals, and anything hazardous. The app tells you before you book, and the driver can refuse an item on the day.

Will there be a driver app?

Yes. Drivers see their day in order, with addresses, access notes and photos, record before and after shots, enter weights and grades for scrap, and close the job with a waste transfer note. If a job cannot be done, the reason and a photo go to the customer so they can rebook.
