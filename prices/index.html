How the price works

Book the load size you think you've got. When we arrive we check it with you before we load. If it's bigger or heavier, we tell you the new price there and then, and you decide. If it's smaller, you pay less.

  • Our driver does all the loading
  • No skip, no permit, no parking hassle
  • Waste goes to authorised recycling and transfer sites
  • Scrap metal in your load comes off the price, and valuable metal is paid for by bank transfer
  • We don't take asbestos, chemicals, paint, gas bottles, tyres, batteries, or fridges and freezers
