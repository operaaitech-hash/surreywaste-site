Questions people ask

How do I know which size to book?

A quarter cage is about a small bathroom's worth, half a cage is a kitchen strip-out or a garage clear-out, and a full cage is a whole house clear-out. If you're not sure, book "Not sure" and we'll size it with you when we arrive. Nothing is loaded until you've agreed the price.

What if there's more than I booked?

We tell you the new price before we load anything, and you decide. If there's less, you pay less.

Do I need to be there?

Someone needs to show us the waste and agree the load, unless it's outside and you've sent photos in advance. Our driver calls about 30 minutes before arriving.

Can you come today?

Often, if the van has a gap. We are open 24 hours, 7 days a week. Book the earliest slot shown, or call 07874 271317 and ask.

Where does the waste go?

To licensed transfer and recycling sites. You get a waste transfer note by email for every collection, which is the document the law requires you to keep.

How do I pay?

By card or bank transfer on the day, once the job is done. Approved business accounts are invoiced monthly.
